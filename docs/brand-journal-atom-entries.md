## Atom — AI Video Generator

### Origin Story
**The Real Steel Connection 🥊**

The name Atom comes from the 2011 movie *Real Steel* — a film where a beat-up, second-generation sparring robot named Atom is discovered in a junkyard, dismissed as obsolete, and goes on to become the people's champion.

**Atom from the movie:**
- Found in a junkyard, considered worthless
- A sparring bot — never meant for the ring
- Small, outdated, no-name
- Had a unique "shadow mode" that mirrored its operator's movements
- Won by being underestimated

**Atom the product = same energy.**
- Takes something simple (a URL) and turns it into something that competes
- No big studio, no expensive gear — just your product description, 60 seconds, done
- AI does the work, like Atom's shadow mode mirrors a human
- Underdog tool that delivers knockout results

**Tagline:** "The smallest unit of marketing" — referencing both the scientific atom and the underdog bot that proved size doesn't matter.

### Brand Narrative
> "In Real Steel, a junkyard sparring bot became the champion nobody saw coming. Atom the product works the same way — you give it a product URL and it delivers a promo video that looks like it cost 10x more. No scripts, no studios, no editing. Just your product, 60 seconds, done."

### Marketing Angles
| Angle | Hook | Platform Fit |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Real Steel Core** | "We named Atom after the underdog robot that beat the system" | TT, YT, Blog |
| **Comparison** | "This took 60 seconds vs this took 3 days and $2K" (Atom output vs agency) | TT, YT |
| **The 60-Second Pitch** | "Can you explain your product in 60 seconds? Atom can." | IG Reel |
